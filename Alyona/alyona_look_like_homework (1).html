look like + noun

Use it when something is similar to another thing or person.

He looks like my brother.

This café looks like a film set.

look + adjective

Use it to describe appearance or feeling.

You look tired.

The sky looks beautiful.

be like

Use it to ask about personality, character or experience.

What is she like?

What was the trip like?
