⏳ Part 1 — Past Continuous

We use Past Continuous for an action in progress in the past.

subject + was / were + verb + -ing
Subjectwas / wereexample
I / He / She / ItwasShe was sleeping.
You / We / TheywereThey were playing.

Negatives & Questions

TypeFormExample
➖ Negativewasn’t / weren’t + -ingHe wasn’t reading.
❓ QuestionWas/Were + subject + -ing?Was Bob sleeping?
✅ Short answerYes, ... was/were. / No, ... wasn’t/weren’t.Yes, he was.

✏️ Spelling: how to add -ing

RuleExample
Normal → + ingplay → playing
-e → drop e, + ingmake → making
short vowel + 1 consonant → double itsit → sitting, run → running

⌛ Part 2 — The two pasts together

This is the magic rule. Watch the timeline! ⬇️

"I was watching TV when the phone rang."

📺 I was watching TV
📞 rang!

Past Continuous = the longer action already happening.

Past Simple = the short action that interrupts it.

🔑 WHEN + Past Simple | WHILE + Past Continuous

Practice

🎯 D. while or when?

while goes before the long action (-ing). when goes before the short, finished action.

1whilewhen I was sleeping, the storm started.
2She was cooking whilewhen the phone rang.
3They saw a fox whilewhen they were walking home.
