1 Grammar Module

have to  /  has to 🔋

We use have to / has to to say something is necessary — a job, a duty, a rule.

🔋 have to

= it is necessary (for I / you / we / they)
I / You / We / They + have to + verb
→ We have to charge the robots.

⚙️ has to

= it is necessary (for he / she / it)
He / She / It + has to + verb
→ The robot has to follow orders.
🧠 Golden rule: he / she / it → hasto? No! → has to. Everybody else → have to. After it, always use the simple verb: has to work, not has to works.

Listen and repeat 🔊

I have to do my homework.
She has to fix the drone.
Robots have to save their battery.

2 Grammar Module

don't have to  /  doesn't have to 🟢

This means it is NOT necessary. You can do it… but you don't need to. It's your choice!

🟢 don't have to

not necessary (I / you / we / they)
I / You / We / They + don't have to + verb
→ You don't have to come. It's optional.

🟢 doesn't have to

not necessary (he / she / it)
He / She / It + doesn't have to + verb
→ A flying robot doesn't have to walk.
🤖 Robot example: A drone can fly, so it doesn't have to walk. It's not necessary — but it's not forbidden either. Just not needed!
It's Sunday. I don't have to go to school. 😴
The robot doesn't have to sleep. It never gets tired.

3 Grammar Module

must  /  mustn't ⚡

must = a strong, important rule. mustn't = STOP! It is forbidden. And good news — the form is the same for everybody!

⚡ must

strong obligation — do it, it's important!
I / You / He / She / We / They + must + verb
→ You must wear safety glasses.

⛔ mustn't

prohibition — don't do it, it's forbidden!
must not = mustn't
→ You mustn't touch the hot robot.
⭐ No -s! We never say he musts. It's just he must, she must, it must. Easy!

🔎 have to vs must — who makes the rule?

Both mean "it's necessary". The small difference is WHO the rule comes from.

🔋 have to

the rule comes from OUTSIDE — a law, the school, your parents, your job
Someone else decides.
→ I have to wear a uniform. (the school says so)
→ The robot has to charge. (the factory rule)

⚡ must

the strong feeling comes from ME — I think it's really important
I decide / I feel it.
→ I must fix my robot today! (I really want to)
→ You must see this — it's amazing!
🤖 Robot memory trick: have to = the factory rule book 📖 tells you.   must = the robot's own inside voice 🔊 says "this is important!".
💬 Often both are OK in real life — this just shows the usual feeling.
AMy mum says I ___ clean my room. (mum's rule)
BWow, this robot game is great! You ___ try it! (my strong feeling)

Listen and repeat 🔊

You must listen to the teacher.
Robots mustn't hurt people.

⭐ The most important part!

don't have to ≠ mustn't

Careful! These two look similar but they are totally different. This is the #1 robot trap. 🪤

🟢 don't have to

= it's NOT necessary
(but you CAN if you want)
  • You have a free choice ✅
  • "You don't have to wear a hat." → wear it or not, up to you!
VS

⛔ mustn't

= it's FORBIDDEN
(you CAN'T — no way!)
  • No choice — it's a big NO 🚫
  • "You mustn't run in the lab." → dangerous, never do it!
🤖 Robot memory trick: don't have to = the robot says "you can relax 😌".   mustn't = the robot flashes red and says "STOP! 🚨"
