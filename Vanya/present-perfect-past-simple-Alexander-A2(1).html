Past Simple

A finished action at a known past time. We say when it happened.

yesterdaylast week … agoin 2019

Ivan played football yesterday.

Present Perfect

A past action connected to now. The time is not important — the result is.
have/has + V3 (past participle)

justalreadyyet ever / neversince / for

Victoria has just cooked dinner. (it's ready now!)
