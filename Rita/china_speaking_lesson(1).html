    加油!

    “Jiāyóu” in Chinese means “Come on — you can do it!”. You've just spoken English for a whole lesson, calmly and with confidence. You'll do exactly the same in China. 🌏

    🎒 Your rescue phrases for the trip:

    • Sorry, I don't understand. — when something is unclear
    • Can you say that again, please? — ask to repeat
    • How much is it? — ask about a price
    • I would like…, please. — order or ask for something
    • Can you help me? — ask for help

    The golden rule: speaking matters more than speaking perfectly. 💛
