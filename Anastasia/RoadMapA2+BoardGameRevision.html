Quick guide

How to play

Two players, one route

Student and teacher take turns. Roll the die and the active token moves exactly that many numbered squares. Every landing square opens a challenge from Units 1–8: grammar, vocabulary, functions, error correction, word order or speaking.

Correct: the active player earns a star.
Not quite: review the rule, optionally do 1–2 mini-practice items, then return to the board. The turn then passes to the other player.

The board image stays completely clean: there are no extra square outlines or clickable overlays on top of it.
