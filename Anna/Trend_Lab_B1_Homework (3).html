01 / Meaning & examples

Notice the change.

Study the expressions as complete phrases. Compare the meanings and read each example in context.

12 phrases
Remember the difference: prices rise / fall; tasks get harder / easier; a situation gets worse / better. The same direction does not always mean the same verb.

rise / fall

Usually for amounts, levels, temperature, prices and numbers.

get better / worse

Usually for quality, health, weather, situations and performance.
