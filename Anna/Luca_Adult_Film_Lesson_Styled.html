After Watching • Speaking
Choose the questions that create the best conversation.

IDENTITY & FITTING IN

When do people hide part of themselves? Is it ever necessary?

Give one reason and one example.

PARENTS & CONTROL

Were Luca's parents protecting him or controlling him? Where is the line?

Give one reason and one example.

FRIENDSHIP & JEALOUSY

Can jealousy exist inside a good friendship? What should a friend do with it?

Give one reason and one example.

RISK & FREEDOM

Which risk in the film was worth taking? Which one was careless?

Give one reason and one example.

PREJUDICE & FEAR

Why do people fear groups they do not know? What helps that fear change?

Give one reason and one example.

HOME & BELONGING

Is home a place, a person, a routine, or a feeling? What is it for you?

Give one reason and one example.
Agree, Disagree or “It Depends”
1)Protecting someone can become controlling.
2)A good friend should support your dream even if they feel left behind.
3)People usually fear what they do not understand.
4)It is better to fail than never to try.
5)Home can be a person, not a place.
Useful language
In my view… I partly agree because… I see it differently… The scene suggests that… One example is… It depends on… Compared with…, this character…

Homework • Choose One Main Task
Write naturally. Use the prompts only as support.
Option A — Film review (120–160 words)
¶1Introduce the film and give your general opinion.
¶2Describe one strong character, relationship or scene.
¶3Explain the main message and say who would enjoy it.
Try to use: although • because • while • however • in the end
Option B — Message
Write a message from Alberto to Luca six months later. What has changed in Portorosso? What does Alberto miss? What is he proud of?
100–140 words
Option C — Compare
Compare two characters. Use at least two similarities, two differences and one final opinion.
8–10 sentences
