    2

    Exams, courses, degrees

    In each sentence, two verbs are fine and one is wrong. Tap the wrong verb to cross it out.

    Example I usually have / go to / make a meeting on Mondays.

    Remember

    take ≠ pass

    take an exam = сдавать (you sit it)
    pass an exam = сдать (you succeed!)
    fail an exam = не сдать

    course · degree · job

    do / take a course
    get / have a degree, study for a degree
    train as / work as a guide
    make a course / exam / degree
      3

      State verbs don’t like -ing

      Look at the highlighted words. Are they correct? Tap ✓ Correct or ✗ Wrong. If it’s wrong, type the correct version of the highlighted part only.

      Example I’m wanting a coffee. → ✗ I want

      State verbs: states, not actions

      We usually use them in the simple form, even right now: like, love, hate, prefer, want, need, know, understand, believe, think (= opinion), mean, matter, seem, belong, own, cost, last, taste.

      Это не действия, а состояния: нравиться, принадлежать, иметь значение, длиться. Им не нужно -ing.
      Но: I’m thinking about a new job = обдумываю (действие) ✓, I think you’re right = считаю ✓.

        4

        Habit or now?

        Complete Kate’s email with the present simple or present continuous. Type only the missing words; the subject is already there. Short forms like ’m and don’t are fine.

        Example I usually walk (walk) to work, but this week I ’m taking (take) the bus.

        Present simple

        habits and routines: usually, always, sometimes, every day
        things that are generally true: It rains a lot here in autumn.

        обычно, всегда, в целом

        Present continuous

        right now: at the moment, now, look!
        temporary, in progress: this term, this week, these days
        I’m doing a degree. Something big is happening.

        сейчас, временно, в процессе
