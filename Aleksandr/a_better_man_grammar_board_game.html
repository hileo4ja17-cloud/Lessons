How to play

Reach a better tomorrow.

On your turn, roll the die. A grammar challenge appears immediately.

Correct: your token moves forward by the number on the die. Wrong: your token stays on the same space.

The game uses 40 adult A2–A2+ questions on question word order, Present Simple, Present Continuous, Past Simple, Past Continuous, time sequencers, and because / so / but / although.

You do not need an exact roll to finish: any move that reaches or passes 40 lands on FINISH.
