Speaking structure

Meet the PREP method

A simple four-step formula that helps you give clear, confident and well-organised answers.

P

Point

Say your opinion or main answer clearly.

Example: Do you like coffee?

P

Yes, I love it.

R

It helps me focus, especially in the morning.

E

I usually have a cup before work, and I feel much more productive afterwards.

P

So coffee is definitely part of my daily routine.
