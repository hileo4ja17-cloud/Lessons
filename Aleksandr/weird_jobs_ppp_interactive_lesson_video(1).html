7. Small grammar bridge

Only a short look today. Next lesson: relative clauses with who / which / that.

next lesson

Look at the pattern

someone who = a person

a job / a thing which = a job or a thing

that = okay for people and things

1) An odor judge is a person ? smells things.

2) Antivenom is medicine ? helps after a snake bite.

3) This is a job ? can be dangerous.

Tip: that can also be correct in 2 and 3.
